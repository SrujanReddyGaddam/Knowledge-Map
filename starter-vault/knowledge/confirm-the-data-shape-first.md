## Context
A discovery call where the requirements described the data in words only.

## What happened
The described fields and the real export differed in names, formats and optional sections.

## The lesson
A redacted sample record beats any description. Ask for one before designing anything.

## How to apply it
Put "share 3 redacted sample records" on the action items of every kickoff.
